About

Not just a pretty page, a business asset.

I started freelancing in 2024, after a period of illness, surgery and recovery which left me out of work and asking myself what I had to lose, so I tried it.

I believe in solid web design and development, so I want to bring agency-level quality to small and medium businesses without the agency price, because the only thing that actually matters is whether the site brings in work.

I do not think AI will replace web designers and developers. It is capable, but without the human behind it, it cannot ship a performant website by itself.
